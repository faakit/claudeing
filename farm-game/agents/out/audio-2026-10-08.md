  the original synth plays for any cue whose file is missing or fails to decode, and before files load.

  Every source file, author, licence and sha1 in `audio-src/SOURCES.md`; credits in `ASSETS.md`. The drip pack
  first used was dropped because its upstream licence could not be verified (critic finding).

## Critic conversation

- R0 (plan): baked 30 s loops would just be the synth in MP3, repeat 20-30 times a day, and cost 100+ MB
  decoded. Resolved by switching to composed note data on CC0 instrument samples with phrase pools; loop maths
  then only for ambience (bodies multiple of 1/300 s, tested). Also agreed: gain-staging table, loudness per
  family, one AudioContext (Phaser `noAudio`, e2e-asserted), resume pieces where they left off, voice caps,
  birds as one-shots, no double download with the service worker.
- R1 (sampler risks): pitch-shift limits (every composed note tested to need <= 4 semitones, 6 for mallets and
  pizz; pitch of every sample measured, several VCSL/VSCO names were an octave off; strumstick and kalimba
  dropped), sustain loops with baked crossfades and seam numbers, CPU numbers, late-bar catch-up, no
  scheduling when muted, sample prep report, per-instrument fallback. All done.
- R2 (review of `8ee191f`): drips licence (blocker, fixed), loudness on the wrong scale (fixed in files, not
  yet re-measured), held repeats stacking (fixed in code, to be proven by render), shared harmony across
  seasons (open, top next step), pad dip (overlap fix, to be re-measured), eviction bug (fixed + test),
  cricket repeat (two layers), SW cache re-download and perf sfx coverage (open), nits (mostly fixed).
