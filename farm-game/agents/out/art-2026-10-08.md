# Art session 2026-10-08 (final report)

Branch `art/atlas`. Everything below was checked only in **headless Chromium screenshots** (1x = 200x400 canvas,
phone = 390x844 CSS px at DPR 2). Nobody has looked at it on a real phone. The next steps are in
`agents/NEXT-STEPS-ART.md`.

## What landed

- **Manifest** `agents/out/art-manifest.md`, printed by `node art-src/tools/manifest.mjs` from `src/art/manifest.ts`.
  Keys come from the data files, so keys added on the depth branch appear automatically (they fall back to
  placeholders until art exists). Landmarks and the mailbox are optional entries; depth's `ensureTexture` draws them
  when no art exists, and the loader now creates a canvas for such lazy keys.
- **Loader** `src/art/atlasLoader.ts`: `world`, `ui`, `chars` atlases plus `tilesets/tiles.png`. Each key becomes a
  view on the shared atlas GPU texture (call sites unchanged). Partial coverage, or the Canvas renderer, paints over
  the generated canvas. Missing keys log an error, which makes e2e fail.
- **Test** `tests/art.test.ts`: every key resolves to an atlas frame of an allowed size or to a generated fallback; no
  orphan frames; tileset shape; roof layout.
- **Pipeline** `art-src/tools/`: Flow crop -> chroma key -> native grid detection -> per-cell median -> palette
  quantization (coverage-weighted mode when downscaling) -> cleanup -> outline redrawn in palette `ink`. Characters
  stay on their native grid; heights come from deleting whole rows. Authored tiles and glyphs come from palette
  slot names. `tiles_extra.py` builds 152 extra map tiles (edges, shorelines, rock masses, shadows, flat decor,
  props, tree tops, roofs and eaves) for the maps pass.
- **Palette v2** (the art director's ramps) with an explicit `ink` outline. Watered soil is dithered so seedlings keep
  contrast.
- **Villagers regenerated** on one body style. Heights: player 28 in all directions, Mara 28, Finn 29, Rosa 26,
  Orin 30 (24x32 frame), Clay 28. Lineup: `art-shots/villagers-lineup-6x.png`.
- **UI skin**: walnut chrome and parchment content sheets, implemented behind `?skin=walnut` with `C`/`CH` tokens.
  Plum stays the default until the critic's fixes land. Mock: `art-shots/ui-skin-mock-{farm,bag,shop}.png`.
- **Roofs** are red clay with a sand ridge cap (shop: slate). Ground tufts are drawn at runtime by `src/art/decor.ts`
  until the maps pass moves them into map layers.
- **Code touched outside src/art**:
  - `PreloadScene`, `NpcRenderer` (villager sheets, 2-frame idle), `ObjectsRenderer` (native-size animals with an
    idle), and `WorldScene` (one call for roofs and decor).
  - `src/ui/theme.ts`, `widgets.ts`, `font.ts`, `Hud.ts`, `TouchButton.ts` and `VirtualJoystick.ts` (skin tokens;
    the plum look is unchanged).
  - `TitleScene` (chrome tokens) and `eslint.config.js`.
