## Full description (max 4000)

```text
The river left the valley seven years ago. The springs went quiet, the orchards dried out and most families moved away. Five neighbours stayed.

One of them, Rosa, writes to you. She farmed beside your grandmother Ilse for forty years, and Ilse's fields are dust now. "The soil remembers," she says. "So do I." The key is under the blue pot.

Tiny Acre is a cozy pixel-art farming game about bringing a place back. It is made to be played with one thumb, in short sessions, on your phone.

WAKE A DRY FARM
Your farm starts straw-coloured from fence to fence. Till, plant, water and ship your first crops, and one morning the whole farm turns green. It is the first thing that changes, and it will not be the last.

MEND THE VALLEY'S BROKEN PLACES
Each restoration is asked for by the person who lost it. Help Rosa open the Old Spring and the creek runs across your farm again, so you can dig channels that water your crops for you. Help Finn rebuild the Weir and the river comes home through town, lifting his father's boat off the mud. Every place you mend changes the map you walk on every day.

TOW TIMBER, BRING FAMILIES HOME
Once the river runs, hook logs drifting downstream and tow them to the slip, tugging them loose when they snag on the reeds. That timber rebuilds the empty houses, and the families who left start writing home. The first of them, Senna Marl, comes back to the Wend.

A FULL FARMING YEAR
- 14 crops across four seasons, a greenhouse, and fruit trees
- Chickens, cows, sheep and pigs
- Preserve jars, kegs, bee houses, a loom and a furnace
- Fishing in the farm pond, the woods lake and the restored river
- A mine with ore to dig and tools to upgrade
- Crafting sprinklers, fertilizer and farm buildings, and a few home-cooked dishes
- Four festivals: the Flower Show, the Fishing Derby, the Harvest Fair and the Winter Feast
- Neighbours to befriend: Rosa, Mara, Finn, Orin, Clay and Senna, each with their own story

MADE FOR ONE THUMB
Tap to walk, tap to work, drag to till or plant a whole row. A single Action button does the right thing for where you stand. A left-handed layout and a calmer, reduced-motion mode are in the options.

COZY, NEVER PUNISHING
There are no timers to beat. Nobody leaves because you were slow. The valley waits for you.

PLAYS OFFLINE
No ads, no in-app purchases. Plays offline; your farm saves on your phone, with optional cloud backup through Google Play Games. Anonymous gameplay statistics help improve the game, and you can turn them off in Options.

Available in English and Brazilian Portuguese.
```
