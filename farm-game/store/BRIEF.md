# Store creative brief (round 2)

**The pitch in one paragraph.** Tiny Acre is a promise of a feeling, not a list of chores: a fading, straw-coloured
valley turns green again because you came home. The store page tells that as a short story arc. It opens on the
promise, the same farm parched and then alive. Next comes the reason you're there: a letter from Rosa, your grandmother's friend.
Then come the moments the valley heals (the river coming home), the people it brings back (Senna on the bank the
evening her house is lit), and the cozy daily life you can live in five minutes with one thumb. Every frame is real
gameplay from the build, lit at golden hour or dusk, with the HUD hidden where it adds nothing and the camera close
enough that the characters read. The frames sit in painted key art in the game's own palette, under one big
caption that sells an outcome. Towing appears only as an outcome (a house rising, log by log), never as "carry a
log". Cozy games that sell well on Play (Stardew Valley, Spiritfarer, Cozy Grove, Alba) lead with a world and a
feeling and let the first two screenshots carry the decision. We follow that structure only and copy none of their
assets.

## Look

- Light: golden hour (18:30-19:45) for the farm, dusk for the town. Every grade is the game's own day tint and night
  glow; no colour grading is added on top.
- Frame: each gameplay frame sits at an exact 4x inside a wood-rimmed frame, over painted key art made with Google
  Flow (Nano Banana) from real screenshots, so the palette and pixel style match. The key art is atmosphere only:
  everything inside the frame is captured gameplay.
- Captions: the game's own 5x7 pixel font, carved like the title logo, large enough to read at thumbnail size.
- Feature graphic: one evocative image, the valley half straw and half green under a dusk sky, with the TINY ACRE
  logo set in the game's font (no AI-drawn text).

## Video arc (26-30 s, portrait)

1. 0-3 s, the hook: straw turning to green outward from the farmer (FIRST GREEN), with the hero caption.
2. Rosa's letter.
3. The valley healing: the river comes home.
4. A family returns: Senna home, the house lit at dusk.
5. Cozy life: the harvest at golden hour and the reel at the river.
6. Title card: TINY ACRE, The Dry Year.

## Honesty rules

- Real gameplay is in every screenshot and dominates the store video. Flow art is atmosphere only and never shows a
  feature, place or character the build doesn't have.
- Privacy wording stays as the critic set it: the game saves on the device, with optional cloud backup through Google Play
  Games, and the gameplay statistics can be turned off.
- Play note: games need landscape screenshots to appear in some game collections on Play. Tiny Acre is portrait
  only, so the set is portrait. A landscape set from the wide captures is an option for later.
