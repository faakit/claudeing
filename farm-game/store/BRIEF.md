# Store creative brief (round 3)

**The pitch in one paragraph.** Tiny Acre sells a feeling, not a list of chores: a fading, straw-coloured valley
turns green again because you came home. The store page tells that as a short story arc. It opens on the promise:
the same farm parched, then alive. Next comes the reason you're there, a letter from Rosa, your grandmother's
friend. After that, the people the valley brings back (Senna home at her door), the river it gets back, and the
cozy daily life you can live in five minutes with one thumb. Every frame is real gameplay from the build, lit at
golden hour where the game allows it. The HUD is hidden where it adds nothing, and the camera is close enough that
the characters read in the Play carousel (5x or 8x on the people shots, always whole pixels). The frames sit in
painted key art in the game's own palette, each under one big caption that sells an outcome. Towing never appears
as "carry a log"; the town rises house by house. Cozy games that sell well on Play (Stardew Valley, Spiritfarer,
Cozy Grove, Alba) lead with a world and a feeling and let the first two screenshots carry the decision. We follow
that structure only and copy none of their assets.

## Screenshot arc and captions

Captions are 3-6 words. They sell feelings and outcomes, never chores, and every one promises only what its panel
shows. The pt-BR captions are written to read naturally, not translated word by word.

1. **Hero.** The same farm parched at midday and green at golden hour, split by the game's dithered reveal edge
   (`farm-variant-dry`, `store-full-field`).
   - en: Bring a fading valley back to life
   - pt-BR: Faça o vale florescer de novo
2. **The call.** The logo and Rosa's letter at 5x, at a first launch (`title-first-launch`).
   - en: Your grandmother's farm is waiting
   - pt-BR: A fazenda da sua avó te espera
3. **People.** HOME AGAIN, with Senna at her door the morning she comes home, at 5x (`marls-arrive`).
   - en: Rebuild homes. Welcome families back
   - pt-BR: Reerga as casas. Receba quem volta
4. **The river.** The river running through the restored town at golden hour, the Heron afloat (`weir-opened-town`).
   - en: The river finds its way home
   - pt-BR: O rio encontra o caminho de casa
5. **Cozy life.** A full field at golden hour (`store-full-field`).
   - en: Your fields, at your own pace
   - pt-BR: Sua roça, no seu ritmo
6. **The town rising.** The Marls' house in scaffolding, at 8x (`tow-house-marls`).
   - en: Rebuild the town, house by house
   - pt-BR: Reerga a vila, casa por casa
7. **One hand.** The full screen, HUD and thumb controls, held in one hand (`store-full-field`).
   - en: Made for one hand
   - pt-BR: Feito para jogar com uma mão

## Look

- **Light:** golden hour (18:30-19:45) on the farm and the river. The grade is the game's own day tint and night
  glow. Nothing is colour graded on top.
- **Frame:** each gameplay frame sits at a whole 4x, 5x or 8x inside a wood-rimmed frame, over painted key art made
  with Google Flow (Nano Banana) from real screenshots, so the palette and the pixel style match. The key art is
  atmosphere only; everything inside the frame is captured gameplay.
- **Captions:** the game's own 5x7 pixel font, carved like the title logo, large enough to read at thumbnail size.
- **Feature graphic:** one evocative image, the valley half straw and half green under a dusk sky, with the TINY
  ACRE logo set in the game's font. No text is drawn by AI.

## Video arc (about 22 s, portrait)

1. 0-3 s, the hook: straw turns to green outward from the farmer (FIRST GREEN).
2. Rosa's letter.
3. The valley healing: the river comes home.
4. A family returns: HOME AGAIN, Senna at her door.
5. Cozy life: the harvest at golden hour and a catch at the river.
6. Title card: TINY ACRE, The Dry Year.

## Honesty rules

- Real gameplay is in every screenshot and dominates the store video. Flow art is atmosphere only. It never shows a
  feature, a place or a character that the build doesn't have.
- **Privacy wording:** the game saves on the device, with optional cloud backup through Google Play Games, and the
  gameplay statistics can be turned off. This wording is the critic's and stays as written.
- **Orientation:** games need landscape screenshots to appear in some game collections on Play. Tiny Acre is
  portrait only, so the set is portrait. A landscape set built from the wide captures is an option for later.
